This needs urgent in-person care — not this quiz

Sudden, severe scrotal pain — especially with nausea, vomiting, or a testicle that looks high-riding or rotated — can mean testicular torsion. This is a surgical emergency where the blood supply to the testicle can be cut off, and outcomes depend heavily on how fast it's treated.

Please go to the nearest emergency department now, or call emergency services — don't wait for a scheduled consultation.
