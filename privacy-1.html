1. About this notice

This notice explains how Dr Prabir Basu and authorised clinic staff collect, use, share, store and protect personal data when you contact the practice through WhatsApp, websites, appointment platforms or other digital channels.

In brief: We use the minimum information reasonably needed to answer your enquiry, arrange and administer an appointment, process payment, and provide the service you request. The automated WhatsApp patient concierge is for administrative assistance only and does not provide medical advice.

2. Who is responsible for your data?

Practice/data controller: Dr Prabir Basu

Privacy and grievance contact: admin@drprabirbasu.com

Please use the subject line Privacy Request when contacting us about your personal data.

3. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the practice, we may process:

  • your name, telephone number, email address and preferred language;
  • appointment type, preferred clinic/location, date and time preferences;
  • booking, rescheduling, cancellation and payment-confirmation information;
  • the content and basic technical metadata of communications you send to the practice;
  • health information, reports or images only when you voluntarily provide them or when they are required in an approved clinical consultation workflow;
  • consent choices, privacy requests, complaints and related correspondence; and
  • limited technical information generated by websites or service providers, such as device/browser information, security logs and approximate location derived from an IP address.

We do not ask for or store your complete payment-card number, UPI PIN, banking password or one-time password. Payments are processed through the approved payment provider.

4. How we use information

We may use personal data to:

  • respond to an enquiry and identify the administrative service requested;
  • arrange, confirm, reschedule or cancel an online or physical appointment;
  • send approved booking, payment, location and educational-resource links;
  • confirm payment status and maintain legally required financial records;
  • provide a requested consultation or related clinical service;
  • route a sensitive or clinical message from the automated assistant to authorised human staff;
  • protect patients, the practice and its systems from fraud, misuse or security incidents;
  • respond to privacy requests and complaints; and
  • meet professional, medical, accounting, tax and other legal obligations.

Information provided for an appointment or consultation is not treated as consent for promotional WhatsApp broadcasts. Marketing messages require a separate opt-in and offer a way to opt out.

5. WhatsApp automated patient concierge

The practice may use an automated AI-supported concierge within WhatsApp to provide greetings, administrative choices and approved links. It may identify when a message requires human review.

Please do not use the automated concierge for an emergency. It does not diagnose, provide treatment or medication advice, or interpret symptoms, reports, prescriptions or photographs. Please avoid sending intimate photographs, identity documents, payment credentials or unnecessary medical details through the automated conversation.

If you send clinical or sensitive information, the assistant is instructed not to analyse it and to route the conversation to human assistance where that function is available. A WhatsApp response is not a substitute for emergency care or a medical consultation.

6. Consent and other permitted processing

Where consent is the appropriate basis, we ask for consent in clear language and use data only for the stated purposes. You may withdraw consent by contacting us, subject to information that must be retained or processed under applicable law. In some circumstances, information may also be processed because you voluntarily requested a service, to respond to a medical emergency, or to comply with a legal or professional obligation.

7. Service providers and third-party platforms

To run the practice workflow, information may be processed by authorised staff and relevant service providers, including:

  • Meta/WhatsApp for messaging and the automated concierge;
  • Zoho Bookings for appointment scheduling;
  • WhiteCoats when you choose to use its doctor-profile or consultation route;
  • Razorpay for payment processing and payment confirmation;
  • website hosting, email, communications, security and professional advisers where reasonably necessary; and
  • government, regulatory, judicial or law-enforcement bodies when disclosure is required or authorised by law.

These providers may process data on infrastructure located in India or other countries, subject to applicable law and their contractual and privacy terms. When you follow a link to an independent platform—such as YouTube, Google Maps, Zoho, WhiteCoats or Razorpay—that platform’s own privacy notice also applies.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as it is reasonably required for the stated purpose or for a longer period required by law. The intended schedule is:

Record typeIntended retention
Administrative WhatsApp enquiry that does not lead to a booking or consultationUp to 90 days after the last interaction, unless needed for security, a complaint or legal purposes.
Appointment and consultation administrationUp to 3 years after the last relevant appointment or interaction, unless it becomes part of a medical record or must be kept longer.
Medical recordsFor the period required by applicable medical, professional and legal obligations; covered records may need to be retained for at least 3 years, and longer where clinically or legally necessary.
Invoices, receipts and payment/accounting recordsFor the period required by applicable tax and accounting law; where the CGST retention rule applies, this may be 72 months from the relevant annual-return due date.
Consent, privacy-request, grievance and security recordsFor the period reasonably needed to demonstrate compliance, resolve the matter and meet applicable legal requirements.

Data may be retained longer if it is relevant to ongoing treatment, a dispute, legal claim, audit, investigation or binding legal requirement. When retention is no longer necessary, information will be deleted, anonymised or securely archived as appropriate.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, including access restrictions, account security, staff confidentiality and use of approved service providers. No electronic service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will take reasonable remedial steps and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

10. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • explain or provide a summary of personal data being processed;
  • correct, complete or update inaccurate information;
  • erase information that is no longer required, unless retention is legally necessary;
  • withdraw consent for future consent-based processing;
  • stop promotional communications; or
  • address a privacy grievance.

Email admin@drprabirbasu.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, while avoiding collection of unnecessary identity information.

11. Children and guardians

The automated concierge is not designed to collect clinical information directly from a child. A parent or lawful guardian should contact the practice for a minor and should avoid sending unnecessary identity or medical information through the automated chat. Consent and verification requirements will be followed where applicable.

12. External links and educational content

The practice’s websites and WhatsApp responses may link to educational resources and third-party services. Educational information is general and does not replace an individual consultation. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of independent websites after you leave our pages.

13. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, providers or legal obligations change. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through an appropriate channel.
